# References

**Access level.** This list comes from research carried out on 30 October 2026, and it says how each source was read, so that nobody mistakes a summary for a checked citation.

- `[full]`: the text itself was read, sometimes through a summarising fetch tool. Check short quotes before reprinting them.
- `[review]`: read through Wagemans et al. 2012 Part I. The original was not read.
- `[abstract]`: abstract, catalogue record or search excerpt only.
- `[secondary]`: described by another source.
- `[docs]`: official documentation, standard, or repository file.

Corrections are welcome, especially from anyone with library access. Priority gaps are listed at the end.

## Known gaps

Not read, and worth checking by anyone with access: Wagemans et al. Part II, Brooks 2015, Kubovy & van den Berg 2008, Koffka 1935, Palmer & Beck 2007, and full texts of Tullis, Parush, Hornof and Miniukovich. Attributions from memory, namely Koffka's "something else" wording and Rubin 1915, have not been verified.

Two attributions commonly circulated were corrected or rejected during the research: Xie et al. is ESEC/FSE 2022, not UIST, and no paper by "Harrison, Chang & Baudisch" was found. Grobelny et al. 2005 is a Fitts' law study and should not be cited as Gestalt evidence.
